Free crypto: what actually works, and what's a scam

You can earn small amounts of crypto without paying anything. Most of it is slow, and a lot of sites that promise more are traps. Here is how to tell them apart.

Safe habits

  • Use a separate wallet with little in it for anything new.
  • A legitimate payout site never needs your private key. Only a public address.
  • Read what the points or rewards are worth, and when they pay out, before you invest time.
  • Search the project name plus "scam" and "payout proof" before signing up.

Where Splyt fits
