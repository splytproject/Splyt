Privacy notice

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Splyt shares ad revenue with the people who keep the site open. We don't ask for your name, email address or password. To run the site and pay you, we do need to store a small amount of data, including your IP address. This page explains exactly what and why.

What we collect

  • An anonymous session ID. Created automatically when you open the site and kept in your browser's local storage, so your points stay with you between visits.
  • Your payout wallet: the network and address you enter.
  • Your IP address. Recorded with each check-in and referral bonus, and the most recent one is also stored with your session.
  • Earning records: your check-ins, streak, who invited you (if anyone), and your point totals.
  • Payout records: wallet, points, amount, and the transaction hash once paid.

We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or passwords, and Splyt sets no tracking cookies of its own.

Why we log IP addresses

Because there are no accounts, your IP address is our main defence against abuse. We use it only to:

  • allow one daily check-in per connection, so opening extra tabs or sessions doesn't earn more, and check that invited friends are genuinely new visitors;
  • automatically flag suspicious patterns, such as many sessions on one connection or one wallet earning from many connections;
  • review flagged activity before payouts, so honest users' shares aren't diluted by fraud.

We don't use your IP address for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell or share it except with the service providers listed below.

How long we keep it

  • IP addresses are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Security alerts, which may contain IP addresses, are deleted after 90 days.
  • Earning records (without IPs) and payout records are kept so payouts can be audited.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and anonymous sessions. Data is stored in Supabase's Sydney, Australia region.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log visits.
  • A-ADS serves the ads on the page and may collect data under its own privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr, the Tailwind CSS CDN and Google Fonts deliver code and fonts to your browser, which exposes your IP address to them.

Payouts are public

USDT and USDC payouts are blockchain transactions. The receiving address, amount and time are permanently public on the blockchain and can't be deleted by us or anyone else.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Splyt at any time. Clearing your browser's site data ends your session on that device.
  • You can change your payout wallet at any time.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, open an issue on our GitHub repository and tell us your payout wallet address (that's how we identify your data). Don't post anything else personal there, since issues are public. Deleting your data before a payout means that payout can't be made.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
